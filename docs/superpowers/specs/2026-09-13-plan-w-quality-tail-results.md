**The two OOMs and the remedy.** A `MemoryMax` cgroup bounds the sum over the children, not one child. A runaway-allocation mutant in `selectors.py` (the site class mutate4py had timed out on at `_norm_with_map`) grew one child to 2.0 GB and, on the retry, five children to 1.6–2.2 GB each, and the kernel's memcg OOM took the whole scope down twice (02:21Z and 02:22:51Z per the plan's Step 2 record; the restarts at 02:22:24Z and 02:23:39Z in `run.log`). The remedy: a per-process address-space cap (`RLIMIT_AS`) sized so `children × cap` sits under the bound — under it the runaway raises `MemoryError`, pytest exits 1 and the mutant counts as killed. `selectors.py` alone ran at 2 children × 2.5 GiB; every other module at 6 × 4 GiB. The gate carries the cap as `--child-address-space` (default 4 GiB, applied in a `preexec_fn` and inherited by every child); `quality.yml` runs 2 × 3 GiB. Cost: only a test that genuinely needs more than the cap is a false kill, and the suite runs under 1 GB. The strict bound is `(children + 1 + concurrent test subprocesses) × cap` — the mutmut parent and every subprocess a test spawns carry the same cap — so the product is a bound, not a guarantee (register row 23).

**The hook escape and the isolation (02:13Z).** A `scaffold.py` mutant with its vault argument mutated to a non-vault ran the vault-hook install with cwd inside the worktree's `mutants/` tree; `git rev-parse --git-path hooks/pre-commit` walked up to the repository, and the vault pre-commit template was written to the shared `.git/hooks/pre-commit` — the mutant was killed, the side effect escaped and blocked every commit on `main` until removed by hand. The gate now makes `mutants/` its own repository (`git init --template=`, re-verified before every run, a symlinked `mutants/` refused before anything is touched), exports `GIT_CEILING_DIRECTORIES=<root>` and drops the four git location variables from the child environment. A `core.hooksPath` override was measured first and rejected: it sends the hook the unmutated scaffold tests install into one shared directory, nine of them fail on its location, and that hook would run on every commit any child makes. The product-side guard is issue #131.
