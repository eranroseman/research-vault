16. **Ruling 15 amended same day (author ruling)**: the digest is authored from the source's **full text, always and only** — the abstract-only fallback (my proposed interim mode) is rejected. An abstract-derived digest is **fabricated facts**, not merely fabrication-shaped (author's sharpening): expanding an abstract necessarily invents specifics the abstract does not carry — methods, conditions, magnitudes — and attributes them to the source under its citekey. Same zero-tolerance class as a fabricated citation; a tag does not launder it. Second rationale (author): the abstract is the author's persuasion surface — what they want you to believe, not what you need to know — so a digest built on it inherits spin as fact even where nothing is invented. When full text is unreachable, the digest slot carries the literal statement "no full text available" — that IS the honest summary, absence made legible rather than silent. Sanctioned route until the `/fulltext` leg lands: the agent reads the attachment directly via its Zotero storage path (BBT `item.attachments`). No reachable full text = no digest, stated plainly — same honesty shape as SKIPPED-before-discovery.
